1. Executive Summary & Problem Definition

Context: Critical life-science payloads (vaccines, biologics, blood products) and perishable foods require uninterrupted temperature regulation during transit. A single temperature excursion (e.g., vaccines exceeding 2°C–8°C or thawing below -60°C) results in irreversible degradation, total loss of inventory, and heavy regulatory non-compliance fines.

Core Problem: Today's supply chains rely predominantly on passive USB loggers (discovered only at destination dock after goods are ruined) or generic GPS tracking systems that fail to calculate thermal risks, ambient en-route weather fluctuations, or refrigeration failure risks before dispatch.

Our Core Value Proposition: Unlike existing systems that merely report excursions after they happen, our Advisory Platform proactively models route weather, predicts thermal loss across transit corridors, and dynamically advises dispatchers on safe departure windows, alternate re-icing hubs, and contingency routing.

4. MVP Functional Requirements Scope

Module 1: Pre-Transit Route & Risk Advisory Engine

  • Payload Profile Catalog: Pre-configured temperature bands:
    • Ultra-Cold: -80°C to -60°C (mRNA vaccines, dry ice).
    • Refrigerated (Cold Chain): 2°C to 8°C (biologics, insulin, lab specimens).
    • Controlled Room Temp (CRT): 15°C to 25°C (standard pharma).
    • Frozen Foods: -20°C to -10°C (seafood, poultry, dairy).
  • Corridor Climate Prediction: Samples ambient temperatures at 50-mile waypoints along driving directions. Warns if ambient heat exceeds container thermal resistance rating.
  • Advisory Risk Scoring: Generates a composite 0–100 Risk Index (Low, Moderate, Critical) factoring in transit duration, weather anomalies, and refrigerated rest-stop availability.

Module 2: Real-Time In-Transit Telemetry Ingestion

  • Hardware-Agnostic Telemetry API: Lightweight REST/MQTT endpoint accepting JSON payloads: 
    {
  "device_id": "SN-8921-A",
  "shipment_id": "SHP-2026-098",
  "timestamp": "2026-11-10T08:30:00Z",
  "location": { "lat": 33.7490, "lng": -84.3880 },
  "telemetry": {
    "temperature_celsius": 4.2,
    "humidity_rh": 48.5,
    "battery_pct": 94,
    "door_open": false,
    "shock_detected": false
  }
}
  • Interactive Live Tracking Map: Shows route polyline color-coded in real-time (Green = Within Specs, Red = Excursion Occurring).

Module 3: Excursion Detection & Mean Kinetic Temperature (MKT)

  • Threshold Breach Triggers: Flags warning if temperature drifts within 1°C of limit, and critical excursion if limit is breached continuously for > 15 minutes.
  • Automated MKT Calculation: Continuously computes Mean Kinetic Temperature according to the Arrhenius equation to calculate true biochemical degradation over time.
  • Multi-Channel Alert Dispatcher: Sends instant alerts via SMS, Webhook, and In-App notification with direct emergency response instructions.

Module 4: Regulatory Compliance & Audit Reports

  • One-Click Trip Audit Certificate: PDF export featuring time-series charts, complete temperature log table, excursion durations, and carrier sign-off lines.
  • Data Integrity & Audit Log: Immutable audit logs conforming to FDA 21 CFR Part 11 and WHO GDP Annex 5 with tamper-evident SHA-256 hash validation.
